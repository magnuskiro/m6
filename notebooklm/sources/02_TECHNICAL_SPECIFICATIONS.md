# M6 Rehabilitation - Technical Specifications

## 1. Building Envelope & Insulation
- **Wall Assembly:** Exterior finish (Shou Sugi Ban cladding), 30mm ventilated air gap, wind barrier membrane, 200mm continuous rockwool exterior insulation, existing timber frame, 100mm interior cavity with insulation, intelligent vapor variable membrane (Pro Clima INTELLO), 13mm gypsum board.
- **Roof Assembly:** Standing seam aluminum roof (Ruukki Classic), 350mm blown cellulose fiber insulation, roof underlayment membrane, airtight interior sealing.
- **Windows & Exterior Doors:** Schüco AWS/ADS 75 aluminum frames with triple-pane low-E glass (Ug = 0.5 W/m²K, total Uw = 0.78 W/m²K). Integrated motorized exterior zip screens for sun shading.

## 2. HVAC & Energy Systems
- **Heat Source:** NIBE F2120 Air-to-Water inverter heat pump paired with NIBE VVM S320 indoor module.
- **Floor Heating:** Uponor Minitec low-profile hydronic floor heating embedded in anhydrite screed on all floors. Individual digital room zone thermostats.
- **Ventilation:** Systemair SAVE VTR 500 balanced heat recovery ventilation unit with >88% heat recovery efficiency, F7 filter grade, sound attenuators.
- **Solar Energy:** 12.8 kWp rooftop Photovoltaic system with 32x 400W N-type TopCon panels + 10 kWh Huawei LUNA2000 battery storage unit.

## 3. Plumbing & Electrical Infrastructure
- **Main Water Supply:** PEX-in-PEX distribution manifold system (Sanipex) with automatic leak detection shut-off valves.
- **Drainage:** Cast iron main stack replaced with low-noise PP piping (Geberit Silent-db20).
- **Electrical Grid:** 400V 3-phase 63A main breaker, Type B RCD for EV charger (22 kW Easee One), Schneider Resi9 circuit protection, Cat6a ethernet backbones in every room.
