# M6 Rehabilitation - Technical Specifications

## 1. Structural Steel & Fire Protection (EI 60 / R 60 Standards)
- **HEB 220 Structural Steel Beam:** Over 65 m² open living ceiling. To satisfy TEK17 § 11-9 (R 60 structural fire resistance), the steel beam is encapsulated in **2x 13mm Gyproc Protect F fire gypsum boards** (or Promat Promatect board / intumescent fire paint).
- **Garage & Technical Room Fire Separation:** Separation wall and ceiling between integrated garage/technical room and main living quarters are built as an **EI 60 (r-30)** fire barrier with an **EI₂ 60-Sa** fire door.
- **Property Boundary Wall Rating:** Any external wall section within 8.0m of neighbor structures meets **EI 60** fire resistance (TEK17 § 11-6).

## 2. Building Envelope & Thermal Insulation
- **Wall Assembly:** Exterior vertical timber cladding, 30mm ventilated cavity, wind barrier membrane, 200mm continuous Rockwool exterior insulation, existing timber stud framing, 100mm interior cavity insulation, intelligent vapor variable membrane (Pro Clima INTELLO), 48mm installation batten, 13mm gypsum board.
- **Roof Assembly:** Standing seam Ruukki metal roof, 350mm blown cellulose/mineral insulation, roof underlayment membrane, airtight interior sealing (U-value: 0.11 W/m²K).
- **Windows & Doors:** NorDan side-hung triple-pane aluminum-clad timber windows (Ug = 0.5 W/m²K, total Uw ≤ 0.80 W/m²K). Motorized zip screens.

## 3. HVAC & Energy Systems
- **Heat Source:** NIBE Air-to-Water heat pump (Installed in 2018, existing operational system serving as the connection point for Uponor hydronic floor heating loops).
- **Floor Heating:** Uponor Minitec hydronic floor heating embedded in anhydrite screed on all 3 levels.
- **Ventilation:** Systemair SAVE VTR 500 heat recovery ventilation (>88% heat recovery efficiency).
- **Solar PV System:** 12.8 kWp rooftop Photovoltaic array with 32x 400W N-type TopCon panels + Huawei LUNA2000 10 kWh battery storage.

## 4. Plumbing & Electrical Infrastructure
- **Water Supply:** PEX-in-PEX distribution manifold with automatic water shut-off leak detection valves.
- **Drainage:** Low-noise PP piping (Geberit Silent-db20).
- **Electrical Grid:** 400V 3-phase 63A main breaker, Type B RCD for EV charger (22 kW Easee One), Cat6a ethernet backbone.
