# M6 Rehabilitation - Standard & Recommended Building Order and Project Sequence

## Executive Summary
This document defines the **Standard Recommended Building Sequence (Byggerekkefølge)** for total house rehabilitation (Totalrehabilitering) at Myrteveien 6. Following a strict, dependency-checked sequence is critical to avoid costly rework, prevent moisture entrapment, ensure structural safety, and comply with TEK17 building codes and SINTEF Byggforsk recommendations.

---

## 🏗️ 9-Stage Master Rehabilitation Sequence

### Stage 1: Pre-Assessment, Permits & Site Setup (Forundersøkelser & Rigging)
- **Primary Goal:** Legal compliance, risk mitigation, and site safety.
- **Key Tasks:**
  1. Municipal architectural & structural permit submission (Søknad om tiltak).
  2. Hazardous materials audit (Asbestos in old floor tiles/pipe insulation, Lead paint, PCB sealants).
  3. Site setup: Temporary construction power, temporary water, waste container placement, tree & ground protection.
  4. 3D laser scan / detailed measurement of existing timber framing.
- **Critical Dependency:** No demolition or structural work may begin prior to municipal approval.

### Stage 2: Selective Demolition & Gutting (Riving & Utrensing)
- **Primary Goal:** Strip house to structural bones without compromising load-bearing elements.
- **Key Tasks:**
  1. Systematic interior gutting: wallpaper, damaged gypsum/chipboards, old fiberglass insulation.
  2. Removal of obsolete 1970s plumbing stacks, galvanized iron pipes, and knob-and-tube/knivbryter electrical wiring.
  3. Non-bearing partition wall removal to expose structural framing.
  4. Hazardous waste sorting and documented transport to recycling facility.

### Stage 3: Foundation, Drainage & Radon Mitigation (Kjeller, Drenering & Radon)
- **Primary Goal:** Permanent ground moisture barrier, radon safety, and foundation thermal insulation.
- **Key Tasks:**
  1. Exterior perimeter excavation down to foundation footings.
  2. Installation of dimpled drain membrane (Platon), XPS perimeter insulation (100mm-150mm), and new perforated drainage pipes leading to stormwater pit.
  3. Interior basement floor slab demolition where required; laying 200mm EPS insulation, radon barrier membrane (RadoStop), and active radon suction pit (Radon-sug).
  4. Concrete slab pouring and leveling.

### Stage 4: Structural Reconfiguration & Support Steel (Bærende Konstruksjon & Stål)
- **Primary Goal:** Structural integrity during wall removal for open-concept 65 m² living area.
- **Key Tasks:**
  1. Erection of temporary hydraulic shoring posts and support walls.
  2. Installation of engineered steel beams (HEB 220 / IPE 240) and steel columns as specified by structural engineer.
  3. Subfloor timber joist leveling, reinforcement, and sistering of aged floor joists.
  4. Inspection of structural connections by certified engineer.

### Stage 5: Building Envelope Renewal & Weather-Tightness (Klimaskall, Tak & Vinduer)
- **Primary Goal:** Achieving 100% weather-tight building shell before interior technical work.
- **Key Tasks:**
  1. Roof strip-down: Underlayment replacement, battens, standing seam Ruukki metal roofing or tile installation.
  2. Exterior wall insulation: 200mm Rockwool continuous insulation board attached over existing framing.
  3. Installation of high-performance triple-glazed Schüco window units and exterior sliding glass doors (Uw ≤ 0.8 W/m²K).
  4. Wind barrier membrane (vindsperre) installation with taped seams.
  5. Ventilated timber cladding installation (Shou Sugi Ban burned wood or timber siding) with 30mm air gap.
- **Critical Requirement:** House MUST be certified weather-tight before Stage 6 insulation and interior electrical work.

### Stage 6: Technical Rough-In (Skjultanlegg & Tekniske Fag)
- **Primary Goal:** Core infrastructure installation within wall/ceiling cavities prior to boarding.
- **Key Tasks:**
  1. **Electrical:** Main 3-phase 63A panel setup, conduit routing for switches, outlets, KNX automation, and Cat6a data backbones.
  2. **Plumbing:** PEX-in-PEX manifold installation, low-noise polypropylene drain stacks (Geberit Silent-db20), built-in toilet cistern frames.
  3. **HVAC & Heat Pump:** Balanced heat recovery ventilation (HRV) main ductwork distribution and ceiling plenum boxes.
  4. **Hydronic Floor Heating:** Layout of Uponor floor heating loops over insulation boards, followed by anhydrite/concrete screed pouring.

### Stage 7: Insulation, Vapor Sealing & Wall Boarding (Isolering, Dampsperre & Gipsing)
- **Primary Goal:** Thermal insulation, acoustic dampening, and strict airtightness control (< 0.6 ACH@50Pa).
- **Key Tasks:**
  1. Interior cavity insulation (100mm Rockwool) fitting snugly between timber studs without compression.
  2. Installation of intelligent vapor-variable membrane (Pro Clima INTELLO) on the warm side of insulation. Taping all laps, window penetrations, and wire pass-throughs with Tescon Vana tape.
  3. Installation of 48mm internal service cavity (installasjonsrekke) to route electrical boxes without puncturing vapor membrane.
  4. Boarding walls and ceilings with 13mm gypsum boards (Gipsplater / Fermacell), corner protection beads, joint taping, and Level 4 plastering & sanding.

### Stage 8: Wet Rooms, Surface Finishes & Joinery (Våtrom, Overflater & Innredning)
- **Primary Goal:** Waterproof wet room certification and aesthetic surface finishes.
- **Key Tasks:**
  1. **Wet Rooms (Bad/Vaskerom):** Waterproofing membrane (smøremembran + duk) applied strictly according to Byggebransjens Våtromsnorm (BVN). Tiling, grouting, linear drain fitting.
  2. Flooring installation: Natural white oak hardwood flooring laid over cured screed with acoustic underlayment.
  3. Custom joinery: Kitchen cabinetry, marble island installation, integrated wardrobes, interior doors, and skirtings.
  4. Painting & Decorating: High-durability wall paint application.

### Stage 9: Technical Hookup, Commissioning & Handover (Sluttmontering & Overtakelse)
- **Primary Goal:** System startup, compliance verification, and municipal final occupancy approval.
- **Key Tasks:**
  1. Final electrical fixture mounting, smart switch programming, and issuance of Electrical Compliance Certificate (Samsvarserklæring).
  2. Commissioning NIBE Air-to-Water heat pump system, hydronic pressure testing, and flow balancing.
  3. Balancing HRV air change rates and measuring airflow volume per room.
  4. Blower Door Air Tightness Test execution (Target: n50 < 0.6 h⁻¹).
  5. Municipal inspection and issuance of Final Occupancy Permit (Ferdigattest).

## 🛑 Common Out-of-Order Sequence Failures to Avoid

| Out-of-Order Mistake | Consequence | Proper Sequence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Insulating interior walls before roof is 100% waterproof** | Moisture gets trapped in Rockwool, leading to hidden black mold and rotted timber within weeks. | **Stage 5 (Envelope)** MUST precede **Stage 7 (Interior Insulation)**. |
| **Boarding drywall before technical rough-in inspection** | Walls must be cut open to fix missed conduits or leaking PEX fittings, costing thousands in extra labor. | Complete **Stage 6 (Rough-In)** pressure tests before any **Stage 7 (Drywall)**. |
| **Laying hardwood floor before screed has fully cured (<2% moisture)** | Floor planks warp, cupping occurs, and adhesive releases. | Screed must dry (1 week per cm depth) before **Stage 8 (Flooring)**. |
| **Puncturing vapor membrane for electrical sockets** | Destroys airtightness rating and causes warm moist air condensation inside wall cavities. | Install dedicated 48mm service cavity (**Stage 7**) inside the vapor membrane. |
