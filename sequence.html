Standard & Recommended Building Order

The definitive 9-stage project sequence for total house rehabilitation (Totalrehabilitering). Fully aligned with Tønsberg municipal zoning rules (%BYA limits, 4.0m setbacks & LOD stormwater infiltration).

01
Stage 1

Pre-Assessment, Tønsberg Permits & Site Setup

Completed

Primary Goal: Establish legal municipal compliance under Tønsberg Kommuneplan, structural risk mitigation, and site safety.

Key Execution Tasks

  • Architectural 3D scan & Tønsberg municipal building permit submission (Søknad om tiltak).
  • Site boundary verification: Ensuring 4.0m setback from property lines (PBL § 29-4).
  • Hazardous materials audit (Asbestos tiles, lead paint, PCB sealants).

Technical Standards

  • Tønsberg Kommuneplan %BYA limit ≤ 25% (M6 current: 21.5%).
  • TEK17 Municipal RAM approval & HSE site fencing.
  • Structural engineer sign-off on load capacity.

Critical Dependency

  • No demolition or structural modification allowed prior to formal municipal permit issuance.
02
Stage 2

Selective Demolition & Gutting (Utrensing)

Completed

Primary Goal: Strip interior spaces down to timber framing studs without disturbing load-bearing members.

Key Execution Tasks

  • Systematic wallboard, ceiling plaster, and old fiberglass removal.
  • Demolition of obsolete 1970s iron pipes and knob/tube wiring.
  • Removal of non-bearing partition walls for open living layout.

Technical Standards

  • NS 3467 Waste sorting rate > 90%.
  • Dust containment barriers & HEPA air scrubbers.

Critical Dependency

  • Mark all load-bearing posts clearly in red before demolition crews begin wall removal.
03
Stage 3

Foundation, Drainage & Radon Mitigation

Completed

Primary Goal: Permanent perimeter ground moisture drainage, radon gas isolation, and sub-slab thermal insulation.

Key Execution Tasks

  • Excavating footings; installing Platon dimpled membrane & XPS.
  • New perforated drainage pipes leading to stormwater discharge pit.
  • Laying 200mm EPS slab insulation + RadoStop radon membrane + suction pit.

Technical Standards

  • Radon concentration target < 50 Bq/m³ (Action limit 100 Bq/m³).
  • SINTEF Byggforsk 514.221 Drainage details.

Critical Dependency

  • Radon membrane must be pressure sealed around ground penetrations before pouring concrete.
04
Stage 4

Structural Reconfiguration & Support Steel

Completed

Primary Goal: Safe load transfer and structural steel placement for 65 m² open-concept ceiling span.

Key Execution Tasks

  • Erection of temporary hydraulic shoring posts on both levels.
  • Insertion of HEB 220 steel beam & column support assemblies.
  • Timber floor joist leveling, sistering, and structural bolting.

Technical Standards

  • Eurocode 3 (Norsk NS-EN 1993) Steel Design.
  • Deflection limit L/500 under full dead/live load.

Critical Dependency

  • Shoring posts cannot be removed until steel connections are torque tested and certified.
05
Stage 5

Building Envelope & Weather-Tight Shell

Completed

Primary Goal: Achieving 100% weather-tight building shell compliant with Tønsberg height limits (9.0m ridge) & LOD stormwater soakaways.

Key Execution Tasks

  • Roofing: Ruukki standing seam metal roof + 350mm ceiling fiber insulation.
  • 200mm Rockwool continuous exterior wall insulation board installation.
  • Schüco triple-pane window (Uw 0.78) mounting + Shou Sugi Ban cladding.
  • LOD Stormwater infiltration: On-site gravel soakaway pit installation.

Technical Standards

  • TEK17 U-value wall: 0.15 W/m²K, roof: 0.11 W/m²K.
  • Tønsberg height limits: Ridge ≤ 9.0m, Cornice ≤ 6.5m.
  • Taped wind barrier (vindsperre) membrane seams.

Critical Dependency

  • MUST certify house 100% waterproof before starting interior insulation or electrical cabling!
06
Stage 6

Technical Rough-In (Electrical, Plumbing & HVAC)

In Progress (75%)

Primary Goal: Core infrastructure routing within cavities prior to vapor sealing and wall boarding.

Key Execution Tasks

  • Main 3-phase 63A panel setup, KNX automation conduits, Cat6a ethernet.
  • PEX-in-PEX manifold distribution + Geberit Silent drain stacks.
  • Systemair HRV duct routing + Uponor hydronic floor heating loops & screed.

Technical Standards

  • NEK 400:2022 Electrical safety code.
  • PEX pressure leak testing at 1.5x working pressure for 24h.

Critical Dependency

  • All pipe leak & electrical continuity tests MUST pass before enclosing cavities with drywall.
07
Stage 7

Insulation, Vapor Sealing & Wall Boarding

Planned

Primary Goal: Thermal insulation, acoustic dampening, strict airtightness sealing, and drywall boarding.

Key Execution Tasks

  • 100mm cavity insulation fitting without compression or gaps.
  • Pro Clima INTELLO variable vapor membrane sealing + Tescon Vana taping.
  • 48mm internal service cavity batten setup + Gypsum boarding & plastering.

Technical Standards

  • Airtightness target n50 < 0.6 ACH@50Pa.
  • Level 4 (Q4) smooth plastering finish.

Critical Dependency

  • Vapor barrier must NEVER be punctured by electrical outlets; use the 48mm service cavity.
08
Stage 8

Wet Rooms, Surface Finishes & Custom Joinery

Planned

Primary Goal: Certified wet room waterproofing membrane and high-end aesthetic interior finishes.

Key Execution Tasks

  • Bathroom liquid membrane waterproofing strictly per BVN standards.
  • Large format Italian porcelain tiling + natural white oak flooring.
  • Custom kitchen island, wardrobes, internal doors, and skirtings.

Technical Standards

  • Byggebransjens Våtromsnorm (BVN) certified membrane.
  • Screed moisture level < 2% CM before oak floor laying.

Critical Dependency

  • Do not lay hardwood floors until screed moisture test verifies complete drying.
09
Stage 9

Technical Hookup, Commissioning & Handover

Planned

Primary Goal: System startup, smart home programming, legal compliance, and municipal occupancy permit.

Key Execution Tasks

  • NIBE heat pump startup, hydronic balancing & floor warming calibration.
  • Systemair HRV air flow balancing + KNX smart switch programming.
  • Blower door air tightness test + municipal final occupancy inspection.

Technical Standards

  • Electrical Compliance Certificate (Samsvarserklæring).
  • Tønsberg Municipal Occupancy Certificate (Ferdigattest).

Critical Dependency

  • Occupancy permit must be issued before moving furniture or family into the house.

Out-of-Order Sequence Errors & Risks

Deviating from the recommended building sequence causes severe financial and structural consequences. Here are the top risks prevented by following our 9-stage sequence:

Risk 1: Insulating cavities before roof & windows are 100% waterproof

Rainwater leaks into raw Rockwool insulation. Trapped moisture inside wall cavities causes rotting timber studs and toxic black mold growth within 3 weeks. Always complete Stage 5 before Stage 7.

Risk 2: Installing drywall boards before technical pressure testing

A missed PEX joint leak or un-routed cable requires ripping off brand new drywall boards, resulting in thousands of Euros in wasted materials and labor. Always pressure test Stage 6 before boarding in Stage 7.

Risk 3: Laying oak flooring over uncured concrete screed

Moisture evaporating from damp concrete screed expands into natural oak flooring, causing severe cupping, buckling, and floor glue failure. Measure screed moisture (< 2%) before Stage 8.
